Their four basic color words don't sort the world by hue. They sort it by light vs. dark, and WET vs. DRY. Fresh green bamboo and dried brown bamboo get different words because one is alive.

BUFORD: I can tell you what 'red' means in forty languages. I have never touched a wet leaf.
